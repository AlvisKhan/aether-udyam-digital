# Public-offer benchmark — 5 November 2026

These are provider-published capabilities, not independent performance tests. Published bundles already exist. Udyam should compete on needs discovery and onboarding quality, not claim that bundling or self-service is new. No live competitor prices are compared with the fictional catalogue.

| Benchmark | Verified public capability | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| Airtel Business | Office connectivity, managed support and productivity options; a callback route on its guide | Explain fit and deployment constraints in language an owner understands |
| JioBusiness Solution | Integrated connectivity/digital services plus self-care for bills and service requests | Bundles and self-service are established expectations |
| Tata Tele SmartOffice | Broadband, voice, security, productivity and static IP; listed location/feasibility restrictions | Make serviceability, terms and rollout limits visible |

Sources:

- [Airtel Business guide](https://www.airtel.in/b2b/insights/blogs/airtel-business-brodband-find-the-best-office-internet), published 18 November 2025.
- [JioBusiness Solution](https://www.jio.com/business/services/connectivity/jiobusiness-solution/).
- [Tata Tele Business Broadband](https://www.tatatelebusiness.com/data-services/business-broadband/).

The reviewed pages do not establish whether these providers offer an equivalent GenAI advisor or instant KYC. We do not mark those capabilities absent. Airtel is a real-world benchmark; Accel Business remains the fictional operator in the case. Any proposed differentiation must be validated with customers.
