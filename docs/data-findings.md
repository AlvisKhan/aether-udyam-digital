## Interpretation

Professional offices are the proposed pilot segment: stronger conversion and lower observed CAC than micro retail. This is descriptive; segment mix and sales channel may confound the comparison.

CAC = total acquisition cost for all leads in that segment / activated customers. MRR and churn are calculated only among activated customers. Neither revenue nor CAC includes a complete margin/cost model.

## Quote barriers after needs assessment

- Competitor offered cheaper plan: 207 leads
- Quote too complex to understand: 206 leads

- Decided to wait: 107 leads

## Pilot targets (hypotheses, not achieved results)

- Professional-office activation: 11.07% → 15% in a controlled pilot. Compare matched segment and channel cohorts.
- Professional-office mean activation time: 16.9 → 10 days; respect catalogue product-specific installation times.
- Professional-office blended CAC: ₹10,695 → ₹8,500; include digital servicing and support costs.
- Professional-office follow-up calls: 3.1 → 1 per activated customer.
- Six-month churn: monitor by segment after a complete follow-up window; do not claim an early result.

## Important limits

Forward six-month churn fields extend beyond the simulated 15 July 2026 date. Use only as a synthetic retrospective illustration, never as an available real-time targeting signal. Dataset segment shares differ from the indicative market shares in the case appendix. Do not equate the two.
